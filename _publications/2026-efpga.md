Embedded FPGAs (eFPGAs) put a reconfigurable fabric directly inside an SoC, so part of a chip can be reprogrammed after fabrication. Most of an FPGA's area and delay goes to its **routing**: the wires, switch boxes, and connection boxes that link logic blocks together.

This ongoing project with Prof. Ken Mai explores **heterogeneous eFPGA architectures** and **routing fabric configurations**, looking for interconnect that spends less area and delay for the same routability.
