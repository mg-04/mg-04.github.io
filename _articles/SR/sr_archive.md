This is a landing page dedicated to archiving my Simon's Rock STEM materials, built in memory of my dead school that still holds countless of memories.

Simon's Rock had been a fascinating place for STEM education, with renowned professors, small class sizes, and dedicated, hands-on guidance.

# A Bit of History
Pailyn (SR 19) originally created a [Physics @ SR](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1fHkioHDRGUjuyxzlewKtyJYpLVtfuubg) collection ([README](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dwUSWxi1ZVQDE-Kg4lzls5GZ5FSXJ8oH/view?usp=drive_link)). *Note: both links are simons-rock.edu accounts only*

Sadly, most professors are gone now, and these exact courses would never be offered again :(

With all simons-rock.edu digital storage and services (Moodle, Classroom, Gmail) permanently terminating by 2026, I've mirrored and hosted these files on Google Drive to keep them alive. You can access the public Pailyn backup [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1CKXXKmygcJeop-VsVFN2P_O3YO4klfDS/view?usp=drive_link)

# Scope
This archive currently hosts all STEM classes I took at Simon's Rock.
- If you would like to contribute materials to enrich this archive, feel free to reach out!

This archive is maintained primarily for personal interest. It is not intended to serve as a standard reference or tutor material.
- I try to keep a relative consistent organization, but there are definitely missing/incorrect/poor-quality stuff. This is a time-capsule archive, not an active knowledge base.
- **Removal:** If I have posted any material that is not permitted, please contact me, and I will immediately take it down.
